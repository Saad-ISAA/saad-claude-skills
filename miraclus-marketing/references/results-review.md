# Reviewing results

Marketing that isn't reviewed repeats its mistakes. Review weekly once posts are live.

## The weekly review

1. `publishing_refresh_metrics` for the period's posts, then `composer_results` (scope brand or
   workspace).
2. **Top 3 and bottom 3 posts** — for each, a one-line why: hook, topic, format, visual, language,
   day, hour.
3. Judge by what the goal was: comments and shares for reach, saves for usefulness, clicks and
   sign-ups for intent. Reactions alone mislead.
4. Write down **one change** for next week's briefs (a hook type, a format, a time slot, a pillar).

## Honesty rules

- Don't call a winner on a handful of posts; say "not enough to tell yet".
- Personal-profile results may be unavailable; don't fill gaps with guesses.
- Compare like with like (same language, same page, similar format).

## Feed it back

Update the positioning file's customer language with words that worked in comments; adjust the
calendar mix; retire formats that consistently underperform.
